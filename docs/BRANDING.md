# Lovely identity

The owner supplied the Lovely Dress Rental & Tailoring logo and requested its colors throughout the existing site. The supplied image, not an invented replacement, is used as a metadata-free 640×640 WebP in `public/brand/lovely-logo.webp`; the app icon is a 64×64 PNG. The original chat image is not included in the public asset directory.

Palette: dark pink #a61d55 for readable primary controls; light pink #fce5ee and #fff9fb for surfaces; #25171e and white for text/surfaces; #27131e for the shared staff sidebar. Pink status/control variants replace the old green palette. Strong red and explicit text distinguish important collision/errors. Dress photograph colors are preserved.

Header/footer, catalogue, login, protected shared frame, preview navigation/dashboard, forms, calendar, categories, alerts and favicon use this identity. Version22 removes the global project/test notice at the owner's request. Internal protection, noindex and synthetic-data checks remain unchanged. Persisted staff pages share the richer sidebar, topbar and card system. Direct legacy previews retain their non-saving notices; they are not promoted in the public header or login page. The source purpose remains a noncommercial portfolio, not a business launch.
